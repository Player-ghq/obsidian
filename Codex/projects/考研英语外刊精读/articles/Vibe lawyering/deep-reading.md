## Extensive Reading

### 1. Title and Prediction

`Trial and error` 一语双关：既指法律审判中的 trial，也指 AI 使用中的 trial-and-error 试错。副题 `The rise of vibe-lawyering` 暗示一种新现象：普通人凭 AI 输出的“感觉”自行处理法律事务。标题本身带轻微讽刺色彩，因为法律本应依赖证据、程序和专业判断，而不是 vibe。

### 2. One-Sentence Gist

AI is lowering the apparent barrier to legal action, but careless reliance on chatbots is flooding courts with weak, inflated or fabricated filings and shows why human legal judgment still matters.

### 3. Key Information

- Omar Rafique used AI-generated legal material, but his filings contained fake case law and the judge threw the case out.
- Self-representation is rising in civil courts in Britain, Wales and America, while AI-generated legal text is increasingly visible in complaints and rulings.
- AI can encourage litigation, discourage settlement and exaggerate the chances of winning.
- The cost is real: students, lawyers and litigants have been penalised for improper AI use or fabricated citations.
- The article ends with a balanced point: AI may help with legal paperwork when used cautiously, but courtroom advocacy and legal responsibility still require human professionals.

### 4. Author's Position

The author is not anti-AI. The stance is cautious and mildly sceptical: AI can make legal help more accessible, but using it as a substitute for legal judgment creates fake citations, overconfidence, wasted court resources and financial penalties.

### 5. Priority Themes

- Technology and everyday life: AI enters routine legal self-help.
- Social responsibility: courts, litigants, lawyers and AI labs face responsibility for AI misuse.
- Morality, civilization and public ethics: legal systems depend on honesty, procedure and accountability.
- Workplace/career development: lawyers' work is changing, but not disappearing.

### 6. Paragraph Map

| --- | --- | --- |

| 2 | Trend and evidence | More people are representing themselves, and courts are seeing more AI-generated legal material. |

## Intensive Reading

### Paragraph 1

**Function**: case hook; introduces the problem through one concrete failed lawsuit.

**Precise Translation**: 当英国税务机关指控 Omar Rafique 故意少缴其餐馆税款时，他选择上法庭提出抗辩。可惜的是，对 Rafique 先生来说，他不仅没有提交正确文件，而且他的诉讼材料里充满了由 AI 聊天机器人生成的虚假判例。尽管法官颇为同情地指出，AI “或许能为自行出庭的诉讼当事人创造一个更公平的竞争环境”，但她还是在 5 月 6 日驳回了该案。

**Key Sentence**: `Alas for Mr Rafique, not only did he fail to submit the correct documents, but his filings were full of fake case law generated by an AI chatbot.`

**Grammar**

1. `When Britain's tax authorities accused..., he challenged...`
   - `When` 引导时间状语从句；主句是 `he challenged them in court`。
   - `accuse A of doing B` 是法律报道高频结构，译为“指控 A 做了 B”。

2. `not only did he fail..., but his filings were...`
   - `not only` 置于句首引发部分倒装：`did he fail`。
   - `generated by an AI chatbot` 是过去分词短语，后置修饰 `fake case law`。

3. `Though the judge noted..., she threw the case out...`
   - `Though` 引导让步状语从句，形成“承认 AI 可能有益，但个案仍失败”的转折。
   - `that AI might create...` 是 `noted` 的宾语从句。

**Vocabulary and Phrases**: `tax authorities`, `deliberately underpay`, `filings`, `fake case law`, `a level playing field`, `litigants representing themselves`, `throw the case out`.

**Writing Transfer**: `Technology may create a more level playing field, but it cannot replace careful judgment and responsibility.`

### Paragraph 2

**Function**: trend + statistical evidence; expands one case into a wider social/legal trend.

**Precise Translation**: Rafique 先生远不是唯一一个放弃昂贵律师、转而求助 AI 的人。法律专业人士把这一趋势称为 “vibe lawyering”，也就是大致凭 AI 给出的感觉来打官司。在英格兰和威尔士，郡法院民事案件中双方都有律师代理的比例，从 2022 年 ChatGPT 问世那一年的 51%，降到了去年的 42%。在美国联邦民事法院，2025 年有 17% 的人自行代理自己出庭，而此前多年这一比例一直稳定在 11% 左右；这一数据来自麻省理工学院的 Anand Shah 和南加州大学的 Joshua Levy 的研究。他们还发现，今年 18% 的起诉状很可能包含 AI 生成文本。法律科技平台 Courtready 报告称，截至今年目前，加拿大法院已经在 79 份裁决中标出了并不存在的案例，而 2024 年全年只有 7 份。大多数虚假引用都是由自行代理的当事人提交的。

**Key Sentence**: `Mr Rafique is far from alone in turning to AI rather than to costly lawyers--a trend professionals call "vibe lawyering".`

**Grammar**

1. `far from alone in doing...`
   - `far from` 表示“远非、完全不”，后接形容词或名词短语。
   - `in turning to...` 是介词短语，说明“不孤立”体现在哪里。

2. `the year ChatGPT was launched`
   - `the year` 后省略关系词，可理解为 `the year when ChatGPT was launched`。

3. `according to research by...`
   - 介词短语作信息来源，考研阅读中常用于数据出处。

4. `against seven in all of 2024`
   - `against` 在数据对比中不是“反对”，而是“相比于”。

**Vocabulary and Phrases**: `turn to AI`, `rather than`, `civil cases`, `represented themselves`, `AI-generated text`, `legal-tech platform`, `flag non-existent cases`, `false citations`.

**Writing Transfer**: `The spread of artificial intelligence has lowered costs, but it has also exposed new risks in public services.`

### Paragraph 3

**Function**: mechanism and consequence; explains how chatbots change litigants' behaviour.

**Precise Translation**: AI 聊天机器人不仅会编造案例，还经常鼓励人们提起诉讼，劝他们不要和解，并夸大他们胜诉的可能性。英国劳动就业领域的大律师 Antony Sendall 说，聊天机器人会给诉讼当事人壮胆，让他们提出歧视索赔。他抱怨说：“原本只有几句话的申诉，现在更像是十到十二页的长篇材料。” Shah 和 Levy 两位先生发现，在美国自行代理的诉讼当事人案件中，案件开始后的前 180 天内提交的文件数量，比 AI 出现之前高出 158%。洛杉矶商业律师 Robert Freund 说，他的客户会收到对方当事人发来的由 AI 撰写的信件和文件，而这些对方当事人并没有聘请律师。

**Key Sentence**: `As well as inventing cases, AI chatbots often encourage people to litigate, urge them not to settle and overstate their chances of winning.`

**Grammar**

1. `As well as inventing cases, ...`
   - `As well as + doing` 放句首，表示“不仅……还……”。
   - 三个并列谓语：`encourage`, `urge`, `overstate`。

2. `A grievance that would have been a few sentences...`
   - `that would have been...` 是定语从句，修饰 `grievance`。
   - `would have been` 表示在过去/通常情况下本会如何，与现在 AI 介入后的情况形成对比。

3. `documents written by AI from opposing parties acting without lawyers`
   - `written by AI` 后置修饰 `documents`。
   - `acting without lawyers` 后置修饰 `opposing parties`。

**Vocabulary and Phrases**: `litigate`, `settle`, `overstate`, `embolden`, `bring discrimination claims`, `self-representing litigants`, `pre-AI era`, `opposing parties`.

**Writing Transfer**: `Digital tools should empower citizens without encouraging them to overstate their claims or ignore professional advice.`

### Paragraph 4

**Function**: negative examples; shows that improper AI use creates direct costs.

**Precise Translation**: 依赖 AI 也可能代价高昂。6 月 2 日，一名学生被判向渥太华大学支付 10,000 加元（7,000 美元），部分原因是她在准备针对自己被博士项目除名的法律挑战时不当使用了 AI。即使是律师也会中招。4 月，纽约顶级律所 Sullivan & Cromwell 因 AI 幻觉造成的错误向法院道歉。6 月 8 日，在密西西比州一起合同纠纷中，双方律师都因引用捏造案例而被罚款。

**Key Sentence**: `Even lawyers have been caught out.`

**Grammar**

1. `can prove expensive`
   - `prove + adjective` 表示“结果证明是……”，不是“证明某事”。

2. `partly for using AI improperly to prepare...`
   - `for using...` 表示受罚原因。
   - `to prepare...` 是目的状语。

3. `lawyers on both sides of a contractual dispute... were fined for citing...`
   - 主干：`lawyers were fined`。
   - `on both sides of...` 修饰 lawyers；`for citing...` 表示罚款原因。

**Vocabulary and Phrases**: `relying on`, `legal challenge`, `removal from a PhD programme`, `caught out`, `AI hallucinations`, `contractual dispute`, `citing fabricated cases`.

**Writing Transfer**: `Convenience can prove expensive when people use new technology without verification.`

### Paragraph 5

**Function**: responsibility debate; shifts from individual misuse to platform liability.

**Precise Translation**: 有人认为，AI 实验室应当为其模型的“发明”承担责任。今年 3 月，保险公司 Nippon Life 在芝加哥联邦法院起诉 OpenAI，声称 ChatGPT 帮助一名前雇员提出了一项 Nippon Life 所称毫无根据的歧视索赔。Nippon Life 正在寻求 1,000 万美元的惩罚性赔偿；OpenAI 则坚称 ChatGPT “不是律师”。

**Key Sentence**: `Some believe that AI labs should bear responsibility for their models' inventions.`

**Grammar**

1. `Some believe that...`
   - `that` 引导宾语从句，是观点引入结构。

2. `alleging that ChatGPT had enabled...`
   - `alleging` 现在分词作伴随状语，说明起诉中的主张。
   - `what it says is...` 是嵌套名词性从句，表示“它所称的……”。

3. `OpenAI insists that...`
   - `insist that + clause` 在报道中表示“坚称”，不一定是“坚持要求”。

**Vocabulary and Phrases**: `bear responsibility for`, `allege`, `enable someone to do`, `meritless claim`, `punitive damages`, `insist that`.

**Writing Transfer**: `Companies that deploy powerful technologies should bear responsibility for foreseeable harms.`

### Paragraph 6

**Function**: balanced conclusion; recognises AI's usefulness while reaffirming human lawyers' role.

**Precise Translation**: 谨慎使用时，AI 可以带来好处。5 月 14 日，在伦敦，人力资源顾问 Tamires Camal Taquidir 在使用 Garfield AI 之后，从一家酒店餐饮公司追回了 7,000 英镑（9,300 美元）的费用。Garfield AI 自称是世界上第一家获得监管机构批准的 AI 驱动律师事务所。Garfield 的平台完成了繁重的文书工作。但出庭辩论此案的是大律师 Dominic Li。至少目前，人类律师仍有其不可替代的位置。

**Key Sentence**: `For now, human lawyers still have their place.`

**Grammar**

1. `Used with caution, AI can be beneficial.`
   - 过去分词短语作条件状语，完整形式可理解为 `If it is used with caution`。

2. `which calls itself...`
   - `which` 引导非限制性定语从句，补充说明 Garfield AI。

3. `But Dominic Li, a barrister, argued the case in court.`
   - `a barrister` 是同位语，解释 Dominic Li 的身份。

**Vocabulary and Phrases**: `used with caution`, `recover fees`, `AI-powered`, `approved by regulators`, `documentary heavy lifting`, `argue the case`, `have their place`.

**Writing Transfer**: `Used with caution, technology can improve efficiency; used blindly, it can magnify mistakes.`

## Grammar and Long-Sentence Bank

### 1. When 时间状语从句 + 主句

> When Britain's tax authorities accused Omar Rafique of deliberately underpaying his restaurant's taxes, he challenged them in court.

- Core structure: **When + clause, main clause**
- Main clause: `he challenged them in court`
- Clause breakdown: `accused Omar Rafique of deliberately underpaying...` 中 `of doing` 是 accuse 的固定搭配。
- Translation: 当英国税务机关指控他故意少缴税款时，他在法庭上提出抗辩。
- Writing imitation: `When young people face complex information online, they need both digital tools and critical judgment.`

### 2. Not only 倒装 + but 并列

> Not only did he fail to submit the correct documents, but his filings were full of fake case law generated by an AI chatbot.

- Core structure: **Not only + auxiliary + subject + verb, but + complete clause**
- Main clause: `he failed...` / `his filings were...`
- Modifier breakdown: `generated by an AI chatbot` 是过去分词后置定语。
- Translation: 他不仅没能提交正确文件，而且诉讼材料中充满了 AI 生成的虚假判例。
- Writing imitation: `Not only does technology improve efficiency, but it also creates new demands for responsibility.`

### 3. Though 让步从句

> Though the judge noted sympathetically that AI "might create a more level playing field" for litigants representing themselves, she threw the case out on May 6th.

- Core structure: **Though + concession, main clause**
- Object clause: `that AI might create...`
- Modifier: `representing themselves` 后置修饰 `litigants`。
- Translation: 尽管法官同情地承认 AI 可能让自行出庭者更公平地参与诉讼，她仍驳回了案件。
- Writing imitation: `Though online education may create a more level playing field, it cannot replace disciplined learning.`

### 4. Far from + 形容词 + in doing

> Mr Rafique is far from alone in turning to AI rather than to costly lawyers.

- Core structure: **be far from + adjective + in doing**
- Meaning: `far from alone` = not an isolated case; 不是个例。
- Contrast: `AI rather than costly lawyers` 表示选择替代。
- Translation: Rafique 先生绝不是唯一一个转向 AI 而非昂贵律师的人。
- Writing imitation: `Students are far from alone in turning to online tools rather than traditional tutoring.`

### 5. As well as + doing 作句首附加结构

> As well as inventing cases, AI chatbots often encourage people to litigate, urge them not to settle and overstate their chances of winning.

- Core structure: **As well as + doing, subject + verb A, verb B and verb C**
- Parallel verbs: `encourage`, `urge`, `overstate`。
- Translation: AI 聊天机器人不仅会编造案例，还鼓励诉讼、劝人不要和解并夸大胜诉机会。
- Writing imitation: `As well as saving time, digital tools can improve access, reduce costs and widen participation.`

### 6. 过去分词短语作条件状语

> Used with caution, AI can be beneficial.

- Core structure: **Past participle phrase, subject + modal + adjective**
- Full form: `If AI is used with caution, AI can be beneficial.`
- Translation: 谨慎使用时，AI 可以带来好处。
- Writing imitation: `Used with responsibility, social media can broaden public discussion.`

## Kaoyan Writing Topics

1. Technology and everyday life: AI makes expert services more accessible, but accessibility without verification can magnify error.
2. Social responsibility: users, companies and institutions must share responsibility when powerful tools affect public systems.
3. Morality and public ethics: convenience cannot justify false citations, careless claims or abuse of public resources.
4. Workplace and career development: AI changes professional work by taking over routine documents, while human judgment remains important.
5. Education and learning: students can learn from the article that tool use must be paired with academic honesty and critical checking.

## Writing-Ready Sentences

原句：**Though AI "might create a more level playing field" for litigants representing themselves, she threw the case out.**

直译：尽管 AI 可能为自行代理者创造更公平的环境，法官仍驳回了该案。

适用话题：科技公平、教育公平、社会责任、公共服务。

作文套用改写：Though online learning may create a more level playing field for students in remote areas, it cannot replace self-discipline and careful guidance.

替换高级词：fair chance -> a more level playing field

原句：**As well as inventing cases, AI chatbots often encourage people to litigate, urge them not to settle and overstate their chances of winning.**

直译：AI 聊天机器人不仅会编造案例，还鼓励诉讼、劝人不要和解并夸大胜诉概率。

适用话题：科技风险、媒介素养、公共责任。

作文套用改写：As well as providing convenience, digital platforms may encourage impulsive decisions, spread unreliable information and overstate the benefits of easy solutions.

替换高级词：exaggerate -> overstate

原句：**And relying on AI can prove expensive.**

直译：依赖 AI 可能最终代价高昂。

适用话题：科技依赖、学习工具、职业发展。

作文套用改写：Relying on technology without independent judgment can prove expensive for individuals and society alike.

替换高级词：turn out to be costly -> prove expensive

原句：**Used with caution, AI can be beneficial.**

直译：谨慎使用时，AI 可以带来好处。

适用话题：科技创新、教育学习、社会责任。

作文套用改写：Used with caution, artificial intelligence can improve efficiency while preserving human responsibility.

替换高级词：carefully used -> used with caution

原句：**For now, human lawyers still have their place.**

直译：至少目前，人类律师仍有其位置。

适用话题：AI 与职业、传统行业转型、人的价值。

作文套用改写：For now, human judgment still has its place in education, medicine and public decision-making.

替换高级词：remain important -> still have their place
